About

Fortune & Flow is an independent educational site. It is not a brokerage, an advisory firm, or a fortune-telling service.

What this site is for

The writing here is about ordinary money skills: saving, spending with a plan, and how compounding works over time. Chinese zodiac signs, Lunar New Year customs, and other seasonal traditions show up as cultural context. They are a way to talk about habits and timing stories people already know. They are not a method for forecasting markets or personal outcomes.

What is published today

Three free tools are available: a zodiac prompt, a compound-growth illustration, and a short Lunar New Year money guide. Each one is labeled as education or entertainment. Longer articles are still being written. The articles page lists planned titles and says plainly that those pieces are not published yet.

What we do not claim

Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Wealth scores, return figures, and traditional sayings are illustrations. They are not measurements of what will happen to your money. Read the disclaimer before using the tools.

This site does not publish a contact form or a public email address, and it does not ask you to create an account.
